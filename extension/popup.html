How it works
  • Press Start, then click where the text should go before the countdown ends.
  • Typing stays inside this tab, even if you switch tabs or apps.
  • Pause waits for a safe moment (never in the middle of a fix). Don't edit the page while paused. Resume gives you the countdown again: if you clicked somewhere else, click right after the last typed character.
